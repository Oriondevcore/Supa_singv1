Features

Everything ORION PRO does. One AI engine. One price. Unlimited potential.

AI WhatsApp Receptionist

Your customers message your WhatsApp number. ORION answers instantly — 24/7. She reads, understands, and responds in natural language. No menus, no keyword matching, no awkward chatbot flows.

  • Understands context, tone, and intent
  • Handles multiple conversations simultaneously
  • Escalates to you when she can't answer
  • Responds in the customer's language

Voice Note Transcription

Customer sends a voice note? ORION transcribes it, understands it, and responds. Works in English, Zulu, Afrikaans, and 50+ languages. No more listening to 3-minute voice notes at 11pm.

Document Reading

CVs, IDs, quotes, certificates, menus, contracts — ORION reads them all. A candidate WhatsApps their CV? She extracts skills, experience, and contact details. A customer sends a quote? She files it.

5 A4 pages included per month in ORION P. Unlimited in R and O.

Multilingual — 50+ Languages

ORION speaks whatever language your customer speaks. English, Zulu, Afrikaans, French, Portuguese, Mandarin, Arabic — she detects the language and responds in it. No configuration needed.

This means you can serve international clients without hiring multilingual staff.

Lead Capture & Notification

Every conversation is a lead. ORION captures name, contact info, what they want, and when they messaged. You get an instant WhatsApp notification: "New lead: Thando wants a quote for plumbing. Contact: 071 234 5678."

CRM Dashboard (R & O)

Web dashboard showing every conversation, lead, and customer. Search, filter, export. See which products people ask about most. See peak enquiry times. See your conversion rate.

Google Calendar Booking (R & O)

Customer says "I want to book for Tuesday at 2pm." ORION checks your calendar, books the slot, sends a confirmation. Two-way sync. No double bookings. No missed appointments.

AI Outbound (O)

ORION doesn't just answer — she reaches out. A lead went cold after 3 days? She sends a follow-up. A customer hasn't reordered in 60 days? She checks in. You define the triggers, she handles the conversation.

Monthly Strategy Call (O)

30 minutes with Graham every month. We review your leads, your conversations, your conversion rates. We optimise ORION's training. We plan next month's outreach. This is why ORION O clients grow faster.
