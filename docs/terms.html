1. The Service

Orion Ventures provides the SupaTraxx Song Request platform. This Progressive Web App allows singers at authorized partner venues to browse our digital database of track titles and submit digital requests directly to the active DJ booth.

2. Reproduction & Licensing Notice

SupaTraxx purchases and utilizes legally licensed karaoke tracks. All files are licensed under SAMRO (South African Music Rights Organisation) compliance regulations. The PWA acts strictly as a search index tool. No audio, video, backing tracks, MP3, CDG, or ZIP file data is streamed, hosted, or downloaded to the user's mobile device. The app handles text catalog data only.
