Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 May 2026 | Last Updated: 13 May 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy outlines how Orion Ventures complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA) in relation to our digital song request platform. By installing our Progressive Web App (PWA) and registering an account, you consent to the processing of your personal information as set out below.

2. The Data We Collect

To provide a fast, personalized karaoke request system, we collect the following minimal data during your one-time registration:

  • First Name & Surname — to identify you in the singer rotation
  • WhatsApp Number — to link your unique singer profile across all our venues
  • Stage Name (optional) — for use when the DJ introduces you to the stage
  • Date of Birth (optional) — to verify venue age restriction compliance
  • Singer History — a lifetime log of songs you request to build your personal Favourites list

3. Why We Collect It

We only use your data to:

  • Identify who is requesting a song at our active DJ booth
  • Link your history across all our active partner venues
  • Build a personal history tab on your app so you can find your favourite tracks quickly
  • Manage lifetime points or loyalty rewards across our entertainment network

4. What We Do NOT Do With Your Data

  • We do NOT track your live GPS location
  • We do NOT share, sell, or lease your phone number or history to third-party marketing companies, data brokers, or external advertising networks
  • We do NOT store or process financial or payment card data

5. How Your Information Is Secured

Your personal profile data is securely hosted on Cloudflare's enterprise network (D1 Database infrastructure). Access is strictly restricted to authorized Orion Ventures administration. Your data stays locked inside the cloud system and is pushed down only to the active laptop of your venue's assigned DJ on the night of the event.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the complete right to:

  • Access your stored data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request permanent deletion of your profile at any time
