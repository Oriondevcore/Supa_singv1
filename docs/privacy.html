Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 May 2026 | Last Updated: 13 May 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy outlines how Orion Ventures complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA) in relation to our digital song request platform. By using the SupaSing app and registering, you consent to the processing of your personal information as set out below.

2. The Data We Collect

To provide a fast, personalised karaoke request system, we collect the following minimal data during registration:

  • First Name & Surname — to identify you in the singer rotation
  • WhatsApp Number — to link your unique singer profile across venues
  • Stage Name (optional) — for when the DJ introduces you
  • Date of Birth (optional) — venue age restriction compliance
  • Singer History — log of requested songs for your Favourites list

3. Why We Collect It

We only use your data to:

  • Identify who is requesting songs at the DJ booth
  • Link your history across partner venues
  • Build your personal Favourites and history
  • Manage loyalty points and milestones

4. What We Do NOT Do With Your Data

  • We do NOT track your live GPS location
  • We do NOT share, sell, or lease your data to third parties
  • We do NOT store payment card data

5. Security

Your profile data is hosted on Cloudflare's D1 infrastructure. Access is restricted to authorised Orion Ventures administration.

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request permanent deletion of your profile at any time
