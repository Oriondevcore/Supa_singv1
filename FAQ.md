# SupaSing — FAQ

## How do I request a song?
Open Zen Search at zen-search.oriondevcore.com. Search by title, artist, mood, or genre. Tap a song, then tap **Supa-Sing!** to send it to the DJ. That's it.

## Do I need an account?
Just your stage name and WhatsApp number. No passwords, no email. We use WhatsApp to confirm it's really you and to send your Supa-Faves across devices.

## What happens after I tap Supa-Sing!?
Your request goes straight to the DJ's queue. If you're new, the DJ will get a popup. If you're a regular, it queues automatically. Listen for your name!

## Can I save favourite songs?
Yes! Tap the heart icon on any song. Your Supa-Faves live at fav.oriondevcore.com — one tap to re-request.

## Is there a profile page?
Yes — userdb.oriondevcore.com. View your request history, milestones, points, and customise your profile with a mood icon.

## How much does it cost?
Requesting songs is free. Tips for the DJ are optional via Yoco.

## Is my data safe?
Yes. Read our Privacy Policy at docs.oriondevcore.com/privacy. We never sell your data.

## Which songs are available?
224,000+ songs across all genres. If it's in the DJ's library, you can search it.

## The song I want isn't found?
Ask the DJ — they may have it. Songs are added regularly.
